Hacker News moves fast, and the best threads drop off the front page within a day. HN Monthly Top lets you step back: pick any month, see the stories that rose to the top, and get an AI summary of each article and the discussion under it.

Browsing is open to anyone. Summaries need you to sign in and bring your own OpenAI key, which is stored encrypted.

**Stack.** SolidJS on Vercel, a Fastify API on Railway, Supabase for sign-in, and Jina for reading articles.
