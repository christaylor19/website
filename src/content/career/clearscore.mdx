ClearScore shows people their credit score and report for free, then helps them find cards and loans they're likely to be accepted for. I joined in April 2023.

**Growth, 2023–24.** I started in a growth squad, running A/B experiments across the web app and home feed. Alongside that I worked on the shared frontend platform: the React monorepo our web apps are built on, keeping it current and its tests reliable.

**CS24, 2024.** In summer 2024 I was seconded to the Credit Health team for [CS24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OmVyt3kKTG8), ClearScore's global redesign of the credit report. The new report is built on server-driven UI, fed by an event-driven architecture on Kafka. I owned the Accounts section across every market, from the first screen to launch in November 2024.

**Marketplace Transformation, 2025–now.** Since 2025 I've worked on the ClearScore Marketplace Transformation, the rebuild of the marketplace where people compare and apply for credit cards, loans and other financial products. I work on the backend-for-frontend: the Node services that build the marketplace's server-driven UI for the web and apps, and I've written more of that code than anyone else. As part of the rollout, I spent two weeks in our Sydney office bringing the new marketplace to Australia.

**Across the stack.** Across three teams, I've been a core contributor to most of ClearScore's frontend stack, from the React web apps to the server-driven services behind them. I like understanding a system end to end, so I'm often the one people bring hard problems to. I review about as much code as I write, and I build the Claude Code tooling the team uses day to day.
