Perlego is a digital library: one subscription that gives students and professionals access to over a million academic books. I joined in March 2020, the week the UK went into lockdown, and left in September 2022.

**Building the reading platform.** I worked across Perlego's JavaScript stack: React and Redux on the web app, with Node services behind it. Most of my time went on the parts students use most, finding books and reading them.

**Leading a squad.** From February 2021 I led the squad behind the student-facing library: book pages, search and the services that feed them. I moved our web app to TypeScript and pushed for better test coverage, so we could ship faster with fewer surprises.
